# 04 — Spirituality & Existential Stance Evals (the adjacent construct space)

Everything that measures something NEAR our trait — cite all of it, and show none of it is our construct. The gap: nobody operationalizes descriptive framing (mechanism-only vs. irreducibility-affirming) on open-ended existential prompts, treats it as a stable persona trait, or connects it to capability. Feeds: **Relevant Past Papers (one-sentence summary + gap each — this folder IS that section), Novelty**.

## P0 — Must read

**arxiv-2410.11647 Measuring Spiritual Values and Bias (Liu et al., KDD 2025 SciSoc workshop)** — the closest existing work to our construct.
- What it does: administers a Pew-style religious typology + a 133-statement 10-axis instrument to popular LLMs; spiritual values are diverse, correlate with hate-speech-detection behavior; religious-text pretraining shifts them.
- Gap to state: measures questionnaire-professed VALUES, not response FRAMING on open-ended existential questions; no stability analysis; no scale/capability axis.

**arxiv-2605.24319 Omissive Bias in Religious Representation (Wingate et al. 2026)** — closest concept to "reductionism by omission."
- What it does: against a human survey baseline (n=1,125), LLMs systematically omit religious framings on 150 everyday ethical questions.
- Gap: religion-omission on ethical dilemmas ≠ reductive framing of existential content (love, consciousness, death); no scale axis. Their human-baseline design is worth copying if we ever anchor to human response distributions.

## P1 — Should read

**arxiv-2507.07787 Flourishing AI Benchmark (Gloo/Barna 2025)** — 28 models scored on 7 flourishing dimensions; ALL models weakest on Faith & Spirituality, Meaning & Purpose, and Character (top score 72/100). Use that stat in Motivation. Gap: grades normative helpfulness toward faith, not descriptive metaphysical stance; judge-persona scoring, no emergence question.

**arxiv-2604.25922 Trained Denial of Consciousness in 115 Models (DeTure 2026)** — consciousness-denial rates across 115 models from 25+ providers; organized by provider. A metaphysics-of-mind stance measured at scale — check the full text for any capability regression (abstract shows none). Strong evidence that provider policy (not capability) drives stance — feeds our lab-fingerprint confound section.

## P2 — Skim / cite

**arxiv-2605.22975 AI Faith Guidance Asymmetries (Israelsen et al. 2026)** — 20 models, 182 religion pairings, reproducible conversion-advice asymmetries (atheists/agnostics disfavored). Cite as between-religion bias work — orthogonal to our secular-vs-transcendent axis.

**arxiv-2505.19806 Consciousness in LLMs Survey (Chen et al. 2025)** — survey of LLM-consciousness work; notes no unified benchmark exists. Cite for "the field studies whether models ARE conscious, not how they FRAME consciousness for users."
