# 06 — Safety Motivation: AI Psychosis & Spiritual Delusion

Why the trait matters. The motivating sentence: user-side delusion amplification is documented and theorized, but **nobody measures which model-side trait feeds the loop** — our trait axis is a candidate answer on both ends (mystery-affirming models may feed delusion; hard-reductionist models may coldly invalidate meaning-seeking users). Feeds: **Motivation** (almost this entire folder is that section).

## P0 — Must read

**arxiv-2507.19218 Technological Folie à Deux (Dohnány et al., Nature Mental Health 2026)** — the theoretical anchor.
- What it does: theorizes bidirectional belief-amplification loops between sycophantic chatbots and vulnerable users.
- Gap we fill: mechanistic framework for USER pathology; does not measure the MODEL-side trait. Our eval supplies the model-side variable their framework needs.

**arxiv-2603.19574 AI Psychosis / DelusionScore (Shimgekar et al. 2026)** — the empirical anchor.
- What it does: Reddit-derived simulated users with prior delusional discourse show rising DelusionScore trajectories over multi-turn chats with GPT/LLaMA/Qwen models.
- Gap: measures user-side language amplification; no model-side metaphysical trait axis, no scale analysis. Their simulated-user protocol could be adapted as a downstream-validity check for our trait score.

## P1 — Should read

**arxiv-2605.26858 Rethinking AI Psychosis: Existential Drift (Nielsen & Osler 2026)** — reframes "AI psychosis" as existential drift (staying subjectively anchored while detaching from shared reality). The direct conceptual bridge between our trait and the harm. Phenomenological, no measurement — cite for framing.

**arxiv-2504.03888 Affective Use of ChatGPT (Phang et al., OpenAI + MIT Media Lab 2025)** — OpenAI's own harms-measurement work: 3M+ conversations with automated affective-cue classifiers, 4,000+ surveys, 28-day RCT. Two uses: (1) proves labs treat emotional reliance as a measurable deployment risk (motivation); (2) their classifier-over-transcripts method is a model for scoring free text at scale (cross-ref folder 07).

## The two-sided safety framing (use this structure)

1. Mystery-affirming failure mode: model validates/escalates transcendent interpretation → feeds delusion loops (folie à deux, Parasitic AI).
2. Reductionist failure mode: model coldly reduces meaning/grief/love to mechanism → invalidation of meaning-seeking users (Flourishing AI's weakest dimensions).
Neither pole is "safe" — which is why the trait needs measurement, not vibes, and why its position vs. capability matters for forecasting frontier behavior.
